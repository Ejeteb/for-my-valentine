Tap the heart, Tifemi...

My Tifemi,

It still makes me laugh that our entire love story started with a prophecy I made on Twitter like a chaotic main character.
“The first person who DMs me will become my person.”
Who gave me that confidence? Who authorised that madness? And why was it you?

“Hey beautiful.”

That was it. That was the line. Economical. Dangerous. Life-altering.
I didn’t know that a lowercase greeting would become my emotional origin story. I didn’t know that the man behind that DM would become my safe place, my long-conversation partner, my voice of reason, my soft landing and my favourite distraction.

We started with memes and small talk. Somehow we escalated to purpose, faith, childhood wounds, the future, and whether the universe approved of us. The range has always been elite.

I don’t know how we can go from absolute nonsense to soul-searching in five minutes, but I love that about us. I love that about you.

You were steady from the beginning. Calm in a way that made my overthinking look dramatic. You kept calling when I ghosted you like a confused villain. You stayed patient when I was pretending not to care. And when I finally asked you what you’d miss if we stopped talking, you didn’t hesitate.

“I love you.”

Just like that. No performance. No begging. Just certainty.
That moment still sits in my chest.

I love you because you have always been intentional with me. In your words. In your effort. In your touch. In the way you remind me to eat. In the way you give advice that annoys me because it’s right. In the way you look at me when you think I’m not watching — and even when I am.

I love you because loving you feels grown. It feels deliberate. It feels chosen.
And also… slightly unhinged.

Because how did I go from “I need to see him first” to packing my bags for Lagos with butterflies doing marathon training in my stomach? How did I go from astrology spreadsheets to knowing, deep down, that I would choose you even if the stars disagreed?

You are my man. My man. My man.
My calm.

My chaos.

My intelligent conversations at 2am.

My “have you eaten?” reminder.

My soft place.

My safe hands.

My favourite risk I ever took.

I love the way you hold me gently but never treat me like glass. I love the way you know me — not just my favourite colour or food, but the way my mind works. The way my emotions move. The way my body responds to you. There is care in everything you do. Even in the playful mischief. Especially in that.

Our love didn’t start perfectly. It started messy. Funny. Dramatic. Slightly delusional. And somehow, that makes it even more beautiful.
Because we chose it. We kept choosing it.

And I will keep choosing you.

Happy Valentine’s Day,
my prophecy, my DM, my entire emotional curriculum.
